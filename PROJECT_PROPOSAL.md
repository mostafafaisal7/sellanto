# Sellanto — AI-Powered Social Media Automation Platform
### Product Proposal & Feature Overview

---

## What Is Sellanto?

Sellanto is an enterprise-grade, AI-powered social media management platform that turns brand strategy into published content — automatically. Built for agencies, startups, and growing brands, it eliminates the manual labor of social media by combining cutting-edge AI with a seamless publishing workflow across 9 major platforms.

> **One platform. Every channel. Zero busywork.**

---

## The Problem We Solve

Creating consistent, on-brand content across Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, Twitter/X, YouTube, Pinterest, Telegram, and Reddit is expensive and time-consuming. Teams waste hours writing captions, sourcing visuals, scheduling posts, and replying to messages — often with no clear strategy. Sellanto replaces this chaos with an intelligent, automated engine that knows your brand as well as you do.

## Key Features

### 1. Magic Link — One Click to Full Post
*The simplest thing you'll ever do to create content.*

Answer 3 questions — your industry, goal, and tone. Sellanto's Magic Mode reads your Brand DNA, pulls currently trending topics relevant to your niche, generates post ideas, writes the caption, creates the visual, adds hashtags, and queues it for publishing — all in one flow. From zero to scheduled post in under 60 seconds.

**Key line:** *One click. One post. Every platform.*

---

### 2. AI Content Creation Suite

#### Captions & Hashtags
- AI writes platform-specific captions (Instagram is visual, LinkedIn is professional, Twitter is punchy)
- Multiple caption variants generated per post for A/B testing
- Hashtag engine creates tiered sets — high volume, mid-range, and niche — tailored per platform
- Emoji, CTA, and tone controls built in

#### AI Image Generation
- Generate visuals from text prompts using DALL-E 3 and Google Gemini
- Upload your product image — AI generates a lifestyle background and composites them seamlessly
- Logo watermarking with position and opacity control
- Platform-specific variants auto-generated (Instagram feed, Story, LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook)

#### AI Video Generation
- Turn ideas into short-form videos using Kling AI and Google Gemini
- Control duration, resolution (up to 4K), aspect ratio, camera motion, and style
- Image-to-video: upload a reference image and animate it
- Auto-generated thumbnails included

#### AI Voice & Audio
- Text-to-speech narration in 6 voices (Alloy, Echo, Fable, Onyx, Nova, Shimmer)
- Adjustable speed, multiple export formats (MP3, AAC, WAV, FLAC)
- Whisper-powered voice transcription for incoming audio

---

### 3. Brand DNA — Your Brand's AI Brain
*Sellanto learns your brand so you never have to explain it again.*

Feed it your website URL, upload your brand guide PDF, or fill in the form manually. Sellanto extracts:
- Visual identity — colors, fonts, imagery style
- Voice & tone guidelines — how you sound, what you avoid
- Target audience profiles
- Content pillars — the themes your brand lives in
- Do/Don't rules — guardrails for every AI output

Every caption, image, and idea generated from this point forward is brand-aligned by default.

---

### 4. Trend-Aware Idea Generation
*Never stare at a blank screen again.*

Sellanto's idea engine scans current trends relevant to your industry and cross-references them against your Brand DNA to surface ideas that are both timely and on-brand. Each idea comes with a suggested caption angle, recommended format (reel, carousel, static, story), and target platform.

---

### 5. Post Scheduling & Publishing
*Set it and forget it.*

- Schedule individual posts or bulk-publish across multiple platforms simultaneously
- Full timezone support — post when your audience is most active
- Post status workflow: Draft → Approval → Scheduled → Published
- Role-based team workflow: creators draft, approvers review, publishers post
- Drag-and-drop content calendar view

---

### 6. Boost Post & Run Ads
*Go from organic to paid in one click.*

- Boost any published post directly from the Sellanto dashboard
- Create and manage ad campaigns across Facebook and Instagram
- Set budget, audience targeting, and schedule without leaving the platform
- Track campaign performance alongside organic analytics

---

### 7. Messenger AI Chatbot
*Your brand, available 24/7 — without a support team.*

Connect your Facebook Page and deploy an AI chatbot that:
- Responds to customer messages in your brand's voice
- Understands images sent by customers (GPT-4 Vision)
- Answers questions from your uploaded knowledge base (PDFs, website content)
- Handles e-commerce queries — product prices, stock, orders (Shopify/WooCommerce integration)
- Transcribes voice messages and replies with voice if needed
- Hands off to a human agent when complexity requires it

Powered by RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) — the bot actually knows your business, not just generic answers.

---

### 8. Analytics & Learning Signals
*Know what works. Double down on it.*

- Track impressions, reach, engagement rate, likes, comments, shares, saves, and clicks per post
- Sentiment analysis on incoming comments
- AI-powered learning signals surface your best hooks, optimal posting times, top formats, and A/B winners
- Monthly performance reports with charts and trend lines
- Platform-by-platform breakdown

### 10. Token Economy — Buy Tokens, Do Anything
*Maximum flexibility for power users.*

Alongside subscriptions, Sellanto runs a Diamond Token system — an in-app currency that unlocks premium AI operations on demand. No plan? No problem. Buy tokens, spend them on video generation, image creation, or chatbot messages — pay only for what you use.

Every new user receives **200 diamonds free** on signup. Tokens never expire.
